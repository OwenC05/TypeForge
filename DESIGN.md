# Design
## Source of truth
Active · 16 November 2026. Surfaces: direct practice at `/` (also `practice.html?mode=practice`), assessment, in-place results, progress, ML Lab. Evidence: app.ts/unsaved.ts/preferences.ts/engine.ts/style.css; the reference-backed direction `docs/implementation/2026-11-15/direct-practice/direction.md` ("Paper and ink") with its three target images under `target/`; the ui-sdk feasibility and integration notes beside it; final evidence in `direct-practice/evidence/` (report.md, manifest.json). This supersedes the 5 October navy/teal visual language; the 4 October rolling-flow contract (Space advances, Enter is an explained no-op, only the current passage is editable, character scoring) still holds. Dated independent reviews and their repair verification stay in docs/verification.md and the review folders; historical approval does not cover a later revision. Browser automation and accelerated clocks are not AT, hardware or human-typing evidence.
## Brand
Quiet, precise, encouraging. Trust comes from working controls and inspectable evidence, not AI spectacle. Avoid fake progress, exaggerated improvement claims and dashboard clutter.
## Product goals
Typing starts on arrival: no gate, no chooser screen. 15/30/60/120-second blocks (default 30 on first visit; the last chosen duration, kind and pair are remembered locally), General always available, Suggested only with evidence, pair practice from the supported list. Results appear in place and Again continues immediately. A reproducible ML experiment remains in the Lab. No accounts, cloud sync, new model or unbounded content in this change.
## Personas and jobs
Everyday physical-keyboard typists practise and assess; technical reviewers inspect provenance, evaluation and limitations without interrupting the main flow.
## Information architecture
`/` → ready passage with focus → timed flow → result in place → Again (same settings, new sentences) or a different setting from the rail. One rail row above the passage: duration keys, general / pair (with a visible pair trigger) / suggested, qwerty; it hides during a run and returns at the result. Why? opens the reason for the current practice kind. Assessment keeps its own page and its continuation links (practice next, fresh retest). Progress and Lab stay in the top bar (a menu at narrow widths).
## Design principles
Native editing over custom editors; one integrated typing region; the active line sits about 36–40% down the page at 1440 and 390 and never moves when messages appear. Every transient message uses one reserved slot of fixed height under the passage. Colour carries state only. Nothing blocks continuing: unsaved results are kept and offered, never used to gate the next block. Honest empty states; a rolling finite corpus is not a promise of novelty.
## Visual language
One light theme. Paper #F3F0E8 (raised #E9E5DB, hover #E1DCD0), ink #17160F, graphite #6F6A5F, muted #5E5A51, context #8C877B (only at ≥22px), rule #D5D0C4. Cobalt #2440C8 for caret and focus; vermilion #C2410C / deep #A3360A for errors and the unsaved line. System sans for UI, local monospace for the passage (32px at 1440, 20px at 390, 18px at 320), display figures 96px (56px narrow). Keys are raised paper with a 1px edge and a bottom shadow. 8px spacing rhythm, 6px corners, no remote fonts, no imagery, motion nonessential (reduced motion respected).
## Components
Top bar (wordmark, navigation or menu), the rail (ui-sdk segmented controls for duration and kind, a ui-sdk switch styled as the qwerty key, a lazy ui-sdk Select for the pair list), the stage (countdown, kind label, why?, passage mirror over a full-area native textarea, restart key, reserved slot), the in-place result (figures, first-attempt key errors, again with its hint, collapsible details, save status with retry/export), the unsaved-results line in the slot, and a today's-activity line above results only. SDK parts are styled through data-slot/state attributes in style.css; the timed textarea is deliberately not an SDK input (sdk-integration.md). The first mismatch carries a vermilion tag naming the expected glyph; a caret-side pointer appears only when the mismatch is off the caret line.
## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA target, not certification. The native textarea is labelled with the passage described once; value, selection and visible focus stay native. Errors use underline plus colour; the expected-glyph tag is text. The slot is a polite status region; per-glyph changes and timer ticks are never announced. During a run the unsaved line is inert (not focusable) unless the person chose to leave the page, when Export takes focus. Real 200% browser zoom (profile zoom, devicePixelRatio 2) has no horizontal overflow and keeps focus and the error tag in view; restart and the slot need a page scroll in a short window. Manual screen-reader/AT, OS IME and complete contrast review remain release gates.
## Responsive behavior
Content column max 960px at 1440. Below 760px the navigation becomes a menu, figures and passage shrink, the slot grows to 80px. At 320 the rail wraps to two rows. The active passage (mirror and native textarea) wraps with `word-break: break-all` on a whole-column width so the two agree at every width (word-safe wrapping of the editor failed the geometry matrix and is not shipped); the line being typed can therefore break mid-word at narrow widths. The non-editable previous and next lines are aria-hidden and take no part in hit-testing, so they wrap whole words (`word-break: normal; overflow-wrap: break-word`, same mono metrics) without moving the active line.
## Interaction states
Ready (focused, or a start-typing key over the passage when focus is elsewhere), running, first mismatch, corrected, committed passage, focus lost (integrity notice, continue-typing key), Undo notice, safety stop, result saved / saving (quiet, muted) / failed (retry, export) / permanently invalid (export only); a failed or refused save uses the notice treatment, left-aligned with the result: the sentence in ink behind a 3px vermilion-deep rule, its keys on one row below. Completed results whose save is pending or failed are kept, in order and uncapped, in a page-scoped list when the person continues: one slot line "N results not saved" with retry save (sequential, oldest first, one write per entry at a time), export (all in one file) and discard (explicit). Following an in-app link while any are unsaved is held once: "N results not saved. Leaving this page loses them." with Export focused and "leave without saving"; returning to the passage cancels the hold; an ordinary second click on the link still holds. The list is in memory only: tab close, reload and BFCache restore lose it, and no beforeunload prompt is shown. Legacy-URL settings fall back with a short slot warning. Duration, kind and qwerty lock during a run.
## Content voice
Concise and specific: General, pair, Suggested, observed, predicted, experimental. "Space advances words; Backspace corrects the current passage." Never invent counts or scores; Suggested explains missing evidence instead of appearing available. No claim that prediction accuracy shows training efficacy.
## Implementation constraints
Vanilla TypeScript/Vite, semantic HTML, native DOM/CSS/IndexedDB. One approved dependency exception: @ui-sdk/web and @ui-sdk/core 0.1.0-beta.3 (exact pins) for rail controls, Select and Collapsible; it adds 18.9 KB gzip to the initial load and the Select popup loads lazily (evidence/bundle.json). No remote inference or key-event upload. Retain edit reconstruction, first-pass timing exclusions and frozen predictions: predictions install at most once per sentence, before any edit, and late saved evidence only refreshes labels and Suggested availability. Finite caps unchanged (16,384 insertions, 256 summaries, 600-character response, 1MiB stored result). Preferences store only duration, kind and pair. Unit/type/build checks and the committed browser suites support local acceptance; independent review, AT and device verification remain separate gates. No lint script is configured.
