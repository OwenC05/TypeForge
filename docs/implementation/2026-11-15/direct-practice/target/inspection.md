## Verdict

**Accepted as targets**, with the departures below. The material is right: warm paper, ink, monospace passage, heavy grotesk numerals, keys as controls. Text is spelled exactly as specified. Each image has one cobalt mark: the caret in images 1 and 3, the focus ring in image 2. Selected and disabled keys are unambiguous. There is no mockup framing. Nothing needs regenerating, because every defect is either a scaling artifact or a detail the direction already specifies.

## How the comparison works, given the wrong sizes

- **1440 targets.** Normalize by width: scale 1440/1487 = 0.9684, giving 1440×1025.
  - Compare horizontal geometry, type sizes, column alignment and relative vertical order at that scale.
  - **Vertical placement is not taken from the image**, because its canvas is 125 px taller than the real viewport. In the running target, the active line's top sits at about 43% of the image's own height, or about 49% if the image were cropped to 900. The binding rule is the direction's: active line top ≤ 45% of a 900 px viewport, aiming for 36–40%.
- **390 target.** Scale by 390/852 = 0.4577 to get 390×845. It compares directly (active-line top ≈ 28.7%).
- Renders are captured at the real viewports (1440×900, 390×844). The comparison is side by side at matched width. No "pixel-perfect" or "1:1" claim is possible or made.
