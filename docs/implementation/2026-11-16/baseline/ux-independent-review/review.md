## Verdict: **REVISE**

The P2 interaction design is sound, and most of it should ship as drawn. That covers the unchanged first visit, the reachable `aria-disabled` Suggested key, "not counted, nothing is taken away", the safe-first reset, and "no focus is a complete outcome". The verdict is REVISE for one reason: **the r1 first increment turns the prototype's central promise false**. A user is told that Suggested needs ten tests. After ten, the UI claims an analysis that never ran ("no key pair stands out yet"), shows `undefined`, and says Suggested "re-checks after each saved 30-second run" while automatic coaching is disabled. Four smaller issues sit alongside it: the summary's semantics, QWERTY friction on test 1, a lower bound shown as an exact ordinal, and the save-timing path. Each of the four has a small copy or rule fix.

## Minimal acceptance criteria for the final baseline-to-personalized flow

1. **Honest promise:** baseline copy names Suggested as the outcome only when the shipped config enables reviewed automatic coaching. `coaching-deferred` has its own sentence. No reason sentence claims a gate that did not run. Reason-to-copy mapping is exhaustive (no `undefined`).
2. **Unchanged first visit:** `/` is focused General 30 s, and the first key starts it. There is no tally at 0, and nothing takes focus while status is checking.
3. **Completion is always useful and true:** count, plus "Last 10 tests · median" (or its pending/unavailable state), plus either the focus with its evidence or one plain reason with General/pair.
4. **Summary semantics:** rolling newest-ten, shown on the final result and on Progress, removed below ten, never cached.
5. **Lower bound:** never shown as an ordinal or as "complete".
6. **Declaration:** one explicit press from the assessment hint, visible at 320 before the first key.
7. **Save timing:** Enter before the save resolves gives the next ordinal n+1. A late save updates only the count, rail and `why?`. A failed save makes *retry saving* the focused primary, visible at 320×640 without scrolling.
8. **Deletion/reset:** count re-derived at the next preparation; no sticky unlock. Suggested relocks with its reason. Reset focus and announcement as prototyped (F7).
9. **Visual:** real-build captures at 1440×900, 390×844 and 320×640, plus forced-colours emulation, for first visit, locked press, test n ready/result, final (ready, insufficient, deferred), save-fail and Progress. 0 overflow or clipping, and no state carried by colour alone.
10. **Labelled limits:** Chromium emulation is not Windows High Contrast, and scripts are not comprehension. A short human check ("what does 7 of 10 mean? what happens at 10?") comes before claiming the flow is understandable.
