# Bounded research provenance

Retrieved 2026-11-15 for the independent TypeForge learning review. Only original research claims were used; no competitor marketing or secondary summaries were used as efficacy evidence. This is a targeted review, not a systematic or exhaustive literature search. Summaries are intentionally brief; no paper was used to claim TypeForge efficacy.

| Source | Access and locator | Use / important limitation |
|---|---|---|

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00140137808931764) | Publisher indexed abstract at [Taylor & Francis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00140137808931764). Search themes were typing practice and deliberate practice, distributed typing training, digraph/context transfer and feedback-versus-retention. The bounded search did not establish a direct randomized comparison of TypeForge-like slow-pair recommendation versus equal-time general/error-focused practice. That is an evidence gap, not a claim that no such study exists. No effect-size pooling or literature-derived app improvement percentage was attempted.
